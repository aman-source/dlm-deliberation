# Dev report, v1.2 (CPU only; dev split; not hypothesis tests)

## 1. Exact objective used to fit tau (Phase 3, frozen before test)

For each dataset d, on the dev items that have all three pre-registered ladder levels (C0 → C1(32,4) → C1(128,16), cumulative NFE 1/6/23) and for each τ on the 50-point grid `np.linspace(0, 1, 50)`:

- escalation accuracy acc_d(τ) and mean NFE n_d(τ), escalating from level k while top1 − top2 < τ;
- fixed envelope E_d(n): for each distinct mean NFE among the 13 fixed LLaDA dev cells, the best accuracy at that NFE (best-at-each-NFE, *not* running-max), linearly interpolated and clamped (`dd.escalate.fixed_frontier` + `interpolate`);
- gap_d(τ) = acc_d(τ) − E_d(n_d(τ)).

Objective: **τ\* = argmax_τ mean_d gap_d(τ)** (unweighted mean over the 4 datasets), ties to lower mean NFE, then lower τ (`dd.report.fit_tau_for_split`). The same objective after pooled per-cell temperature scaling of the margins gives the second τ.

Result: τ = 0.0204 raw (mean gap 0.0046, mean NFE 1.046); τ = 0.0000 after TS (mean gap 0.0000, mean NFE 1.000).

Note: the v1.2 H3 test uses the monotone (running-max) frontier below, which is at least as high as the envelope used for this fit.
